# Product Positioning

Several mature commercial solutions already exist in the regulatory intelligence space, including Clarivate, Dr. Evidence, IQVIA, and Veeva. These companies have invested for many years in proprietary regulatory databases, enterprise workflows, and integrations, and already provide AI-assisted search, monitoring, and regulatory intelligence.

DrugDev-AI is **not intended to compete directly** with these enterprise platforms.

Instead, it focuses on a different value proposition:

> **An AI copilot for understanding drug development.**

### Differentiation

The key differentiator is the combination of:

- **Expert explanation** (Ask)
- **Structured learning** (Learn)
- **Live public regulatory intelligence** (Monitor)

rather than enterprise regulatory operations or document management.

Instead of answering:

> *"Where can I find this regulation?"*

DrugDev-AI answers:

> *"What does this regulation mean?"*

For example:

- *Explain why the FDA requested another Phase III trial.*
- *Compare FDA and EMA approaches.*
- *Why is this guidance important?*
- *Teach me Quality Risk Management.*

While enterprise Regulatory Intelligence platforms assume users are already regulatory experts, DrugDev-AI is designed to **help professionals become experts**.

### Target Users

DrugDev-AI is particularly well suited for:

- New Regulatory Affairs professionals
- Small biotechnology companies
- CROs
- Regulatory consultants
- Researchers
- PhD students
- Universities
- Academic laboratories

These users often cannot justify enterprise regulatory intelligence platforms, which may cost tens or even hundreds of thousands of dollars per year.

### The Current Workflow

Today, many professionals use multiple independent tools:

- one platform for regulatory monitoring,
- another for regulatory documentation,
- ChatGPT for explanations,
- a separate learning platform for onboarding.

DrugDev-AI brings these activities together in a single workflow:

> **Discover → Understand → Learn**

- **Monitor** discovers new regulatory developments.
- **Ask** explains their meaning and regulatory context.
- **Learn** builds structured knowledge through guided lessons and assessments.

### Vision

Existing enterprise platforms excel at regulatory operations, document management, and intelligence gathering.

DrugDev-AI aims to complement these capabilities by providing an integrated AI assistant that helps users:

- discover relevant regulatory developments,
- understand them in context,
- and systematically build expertise in pharmaceutical drug development and regulatory science.


## DrugDev-AI says:

**Discover. Understand. Learn.**

None of the current enterprise products really integrate those three workflows 
into one user experience. They excel at regulatory operations and intelligence, 
but they are not designed as an integrated assistant that helps users discover 
new information, understand it in context, and systematically build expertise.#
